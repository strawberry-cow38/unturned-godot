## Measurement convention and geometry audit

`positions` are stored in mesh bind coordinates. They are **not a Godot world-space AABB**. The extraction path in `tools/deer_rig_extract.py` and `tools/rig_extract.py` stores two weights per vertex and six skin binds for seven skeleton bones. For each vertex this script computes `sum(weight * global_bone_pose[skin[skin_index].bone] * skin_bind * position)`, matching `RiggedCharacter.BuildFrom`. Quaternions are normalized; hierarchy transforms are composed parent first. Local facing is −X, up is +Y, width is Z after skinning.

The main tables use the skeleton rest pose, at agent position (0,0,0), without a foot lift. “Withers” is the highest front-half Spine vertex above the terrain plane Y=0; body length/width are the Spine bounds. “Exposed leg” is the Left_Front weighted vertex Y span, not the hip-bone length (this fleet has one bone per leg). Head core is the position-welded Skull component containing the most forward muzzle vertex; separate eyes, ears and deer antlers are excluded. These are reproducible mesh landmarks, not inferred biological measurements.

**Origin-at-feet caveat:** the origin is the intended ground plane, but source hoof tips extend below it. Tables retain this measured penetration; they do not add the obsolete 0.70/0.22/0.52 m lifts. Foot-tip-to-withers measurements are listed separately.
